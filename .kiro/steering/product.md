# Product

The HR Portal is Megawide's internal employee intranet — a web application that
centralizes company communications, HR resources, and social engagement for staff.

## Core Features

- **Company content**: Meganews (news), MegaTrivia, MegaGoodVibes, Megagram
  (photo/social feed), blogs, and a community board / forum (Timeline).
- **HR resources**: announcements, new hires, job vacancies, HMO announcements,
  demographics, how-tos, FAQs, Megawide University, and "did you know" content.
- **Company info**: corporate offices, company events, holidays, photo gallery,
  and "who we are" / business-and-subsidiaries pages.
- **Surveys**: create surveys with questions/choices and collect employee answers.

## Users & Access

- Employees authenticate with their Microsoft 365 (Office 365) accounts via
  Microsoft Graph — there is no local username/password system.
- The app has a public-facing employee experience plus admin/management screens
  (under `/main/...`) for creating and editing content.

When adding features, keep the intranet audience in mind: content is internal,
employee-facing, and organized around the categories above.
